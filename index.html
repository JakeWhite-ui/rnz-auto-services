Questions

Asked before you call.

When are you open?

Saturday to Thursday, 09:00 to 23:00. Friday we open later, at 15:00, and close at 23:00 as usual. Seven days a week.

Where exactly are you?

36th a St - Ras Al Khor Ind. Second - Ras Al Khor Industrial Area 2 - Dubai. The plus code is 59J9+77, which drops a pin on the gate. Look for the yellow board that says CAR WASH and CAR REPAIR.

What can you actually do here?

Thirteen things, all on the board: car wash, steam wash, car detailing, car polish, car repair, mechanical repair, electric repair, A/C repair, oil change, brakepads, denting and painting, window tinting, and typing services.

Will you tell me what is wrong before you start?

Yes — it is the thing customers single out in reviews. Our supervisor Mr. Allwin and mechanic Mr. Rauf go through the fault with you in detail first, and nothing is opened until you agree.

What does it cost?

We quote once we have looked at the car, because a price given over the phone for a fault nobody has seen is a guess. Call 055 616 6878 or send the car details on WhatsApp and we will tell you what we need to check.

Do you work on big or expensive cars?

Our own Google photos show a Rolls-Royce, a Range Rover, a Land Cruiser, a GMC, a Mercedes and a BMW in the bays. Bring the car.

Finished work and the car still needs looking at?

We are here till 23:00, seven days a week. Drive in, or send a message and we will be expecting you.
